The Map Recorder gives you the capacity to record, edit, and visualize routes,
field maps, and zone boundaries. It is the central hub for teaching your Amiga
where to go, as well as the basis for future job executions on the
[**Job Manager**](/docs/apps/job_manager/job-manager.md) App.
![Screenshot from 2025-06-19 15-35-50](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/26fb2a27-f6ad-4172-b81a-92775224cc5f)

### Path Recording

![Screenshot from 2025-06-19 15-36-10](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e89f60f3-d2a0-461a-ae7e-fc28b4cdd821)
In order to start recording a new route, press the **Record** button and
drive your Amiga along the desired path. This will begin to create a GPS
path. Press the **Pause** button to temporarily stop your recording and
**Resume** to continue with your paused recording.
If you find the need to delete parts of your ongoing recording,
press the **Undo Last** button to removes the last track point created.
**Abort** will cancel the entire recording session.
If you are Satisfied with your recording, press **Save**
to bring up a pop up to name and save your recorded path/zone.

### Zone Recording

![Screenshot from 2025-06-19 15-37-31](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1823c098-ed6a-469b-965c-ffc1faa1ef50)

If you wish to create zone boundaries while you are recording a path,
press the **Mark Location** button to add a waypoint at current GPS position.
Use this feature to mark the beginning of your **Farming Zone** and other
field locations of interest. **Undo Zone** will remove the last recorded zone segment.

### Zone Types

![zonetype](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/308ba613-14c2-4996-ab9f-f698294cb5ef)
There are 3 zone types to keep in mind. The **Transition Zone** (highlighted in yellow)
is used to engage and disengage your Amiga's tools and implements.
The **Farming Zone** (highlighted in light Green) is the zone where your tool/implement
will be fully engaged. Finally the **Safety Zone** (highlighted
in purple) is the area where your Amiga will operate during Job execution.
If for some reason your Amiga leaves this zone while executing a job, it
will be automatically stopped.

### Visualize

The **Visualize** button brings up a list of all your saved **Zones** and **Routes**.
Select a zone or a route to bring it up to your map's preview.
Press the **Hide** button to close the panel.

### Studio

Press on the **Studio** button to switch between viewing **Paths**,
farming **Zones**, and **Safety** zones. Each item can be delete by
pressing the **trash** icon. Click the **Modify** button to edit your
existing paths/zones. Press **Save** or **Cancel** to discard any changes.
The **Create** button enables you to drawing new zones.
**Save** brings up a pop up for custom naming of your newly created zone(s).
**Cancel** takes you out of the editor.
